Construction and final

Blue: 4-unit grid, stronger every 8. Magenta: margin box at 8. Red: S ink edges, S top and baseline, rule top and bottom, centre axis.

DecisionValue and reason
LetterNewsreader italic S, weight 700, optical size 24. The wordmark runs at 500; the mark is heavier so the thin strokes survive 16px.
S height46.5 of 64, serif tips and overshoots included.
Rule6 thick, the S standing on it (0.3 overlap, no gap), spanning the S's ink width exactly. Ends lean 17 degrees, the italic angle, so the rule belongs to the letter. In one colour the pair is a single solid shape, like a stake planted in its line.
Optical liftTop margin 6.4, bottom 5.9. The S carries the weight, so the pair sits half a unit low of true centre.
CornersAll sharp, like the wordmark's serifs and the agreement's rule.
