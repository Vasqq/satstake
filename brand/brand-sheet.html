SatStake brand sheet

An italic S standing on the signature rule, set beside the wordmark the site already uses. One colour pair, light and dark.

Colour

Ink#14213D · rgb(20, 33, 61)S and Sat, on light
Orange#E86A12 · rgb(232, 106, 18)rule and Stake, on light
Paper#F7F8FA · rgb(247, 248, 250)light ground
Ink, reversed#E3E9F5 · rgb(227, 233, 245)S and Sat, on dark
Orange, reversed#FF9549 · rgb(255, 149, 73)rule and Stake, on dark
Ground#0C0F15 · rgb(12, 15, 21)dark ground
Rule on light tile#C4560B · rgb(196, 86, 11)only on the reversed tile

Contrast: S on paper 15.0:1, on dark 15.8:1. The orange rule on paper is 3.0:1, enough for a graphic but not for small text, so Stake in the wordmark is never set below 24px on paper. On dark, orange is 8.8:1.

Assets

Clear space

Keep three rule thicknesses (the height of the orange rule, times three) clear on every side. The dashed box shows it on the horizontal lockup.

mark 16px, 5 mm
32px
lockup 96px, 25 mm
160px
stacked 72px, 20 mm

Below 16px use the tile icon, not the bare mark. Below 96px wide, use the mark alone.
